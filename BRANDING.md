# TechStack Sydney — About and brand

**Dream Code Deploy.**

## About

TechStack Sydney is a Sydney-based web and mobile app studio working with small and medium businesses across Australia. We design, build and support bespoke software around the way each business works. That could mean replacing a spreadsheet, connecting tools a team already uses, or giving customers a simpler way to book and manage their details.

## What clients can expect

- **A clear start.** Begin with a free, no-obligation 30-minute discovery call. We explain the options in plain language and provide a fixed-price quote before work begins.
- **A build clients can follow.** We start with what the client wants to change, set out what comes next, show the build as it takes shape, refine it around day-to-day work and prepare for launch.
- **Support after launch.** Every project includes an ongoing support plan. The team that built the software manages hosting, maintenance and technical support end to end. We can help identify bottlenecks, add features as the business changes and automate repeat admin.
- **Ownership.** The site states that the client owns the source code, designs and intellectual property once the project is paid for. Use the project agreement for specific terms.

## Services and product

We build websites, web apps and mobile apps. Our services include UX/UI design, systems integration, AI and workflow automation, and managed hosting and support.

**DojoSwyft** is our first product, built for combat sports and other class-based gyms. It covers timetables, reservations, attendance, memberships, and staff and member access. See the [DojoSwyft page](https://techstacksydney.com/gym-app).

The homepage leads with **“Bespoke software that fits the way your business works”** and frames post-launch support as **“You’ve launched. We’re still here.”** The primary CTA is **“Book a free consult.”**

## Voice and presentation

- Use Australian English and speak directly to the business owner. Lead with the everyday problem and the practical outcome, such as fewer follow-ups, easier bookings or a clearer view of what needs doing.
- Keep copy direct, reassuring and specific. Explain technical terms when they matter to the decision.
- Keep the light site layout, strong dark typography and focused calls to action. Use the darker interactive panels to show how the work might look in practice.
- Label demo interfaces, industry mock-ups and sample data as illustrative. Verify any client logos, testimonials, delivery estimates, uptime figures or performance numbers before presenting them as evidence or commitments.
