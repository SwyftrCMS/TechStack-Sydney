How we build

From a simple thought to software that fits

You don’t need to have it all figured out before we talk. We’ll help you explore what’s possible, explain your options in plain language and map out what happens next. Here’s how we turn your idea into something built around the way you work.

  1. Start with what you’d change

    Tell us what keeps slowing you down or what you wish your customers could do more easily. We’ll explore what could work better.

  2. Know what comes next

    We’ll agree on what to build first and when you’ll get to see it, so you know what you’re saying yes to.

  3. See your idea take shape

    Try a working version as we build it. Tell us what feels right and what would make it easier to use.

  4. Make it fit your day

    Where it makes sense, we’ll connect the tools you already use so your team can spend less time entering the same details twice.

  5. Go live with confidence

    We’ll walk through your everyday tasks together and agree when you’re ready to launch.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How much does a custom app cost?

Every project is different, so we start with a free discovery call and give you a fixed-price quote before any work begins. No surprise invoices.

How long does a typical project take?

Most SME projects go from discovery to launch in 8–16 weeks. We deliver in fortnightly sprints, so you see working software early and often.

Who owns the code and intellectual property?

You do. Once the project is paid for, the source code, designs and IP belong to your business.

Can you connect to the tools we already use?

Yes. We regularly integrate with Xero, MYOB, Shopify, HubSpot, Microsoft 365 and most systems that offer an API.

What happens after launch?

We offer ongoing support, hosting and improvement plans from our Australian-based team, so your software keeps working and growing with your business.
