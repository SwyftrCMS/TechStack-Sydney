FAQ

Questions, answered

How much does a custom app cost?

Every project is different, so we start with a free discovery call and give you a fixed-price quote before any work begins. No surprise invoices.

How long does a typical project take?

Most SME projects go from discovery to launch in 8–16 weeks. We deliver in fortnightly sprints, so you see working software early and often.

Who owns the code and intellectual property?

You do. Once the project is paid for, the source code, designs and IP belong to your business.

Can you connect to the tools we already use?

Yes. We regularly integrate with Xero, MYOB, Shopify, HubSpot, Microsoft 365 and most systems that offer an API.

What happens after launch?

We offer ongoing support, hosting and improvement plans from our Australian-based team, so your software keeps working and growing with your business.
