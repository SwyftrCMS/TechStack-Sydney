How we build

From idea to launch, without the guesswork

A proven, transparent process that keeps your project on time and on budget.

  1. Discovery & Strategy

    We map how your team works, agree on what the software needs to do, and give you a fixed-price plan to review and sign off.

  2. Sprint Planning

    We plan the build in fortnightly sprints so you know what’s coming and when you can review working software.

  3. Design & Build

    You see working software during the build and can give feedback as it takes shape.

  4. Integrations

    We connect the tools you already use. For example, signing off a job can create a draft invoice in Xero and keep your job record in sync.

  5. Testing & Launch

    We test your everyday workflows, review them with your team, and agree when you’re ready to go live.

Support after launch

You’ve launched. We’re still here.

The team that built your software is the team you’ll turn to after launch. Every project comes with an ongoing support plan. We look after hosting, maintenance and technical support from end to end, so when something needs attention, you know exactly who to call.

  1. Hosting & Monitoring

    We host your software in Australia and monitor for outages.

    We keep backups so your data can be restored if something goes wrong.

  2. Support & Maintenance

    Reach the team that built your software for fixes, updates, and security patches.

  3. Analytics & Insights

    See where work is getting stuck, from quotes awaiting follow-up to invoices still unpaid.

  4. New Features

    As your business changes, we can add features in planned releases. A client portal, for example, can let customers check progress and sign off online.

  5. Automation

    When a job is signed off, a workflow can check the job details and Xero rates, then send the customer a receipt through Sender.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How much does a custom app cost?

Every project is different, so we start with a free discovery call and give you a fixed-price quote before any work begins. No surprise invoices.

How long does a typical project take?

Most SME projects go from discovery to launch in 8–16 weeks. We deliver in fortnightly sprints, so you see working software early and often.

Who owns the code and intellectual property?

You do. Once the project is paid for, the source code, designs and IP belong to your business.

Can you connect to the tools we already use?

Yes. We regularly integrate with Xero, MYOB, Shopify, HubSpot, Microsoft 365 and most systems that offer an API.

What happens after launch?

We offer ongoing support, hosting and improvement plans from our Australian-based team, so your software keeps working and growing with your business.
