Coaching Offerings

Three structured pathways designed for every season of a woman's life.

01

Private Strength & Structural Conditioning

Individualised resistance training built around your joint mechanics, energy rhythm, and long-term bone density—without high-cortisol burnout.

  • Custom 12-week progressive lifting blocks
  • Full hip, spine, and shoulder mobility screening
  • Weekly load adjustments synced to your cycle & stress

02

Postpartum & Pelvic Core Rebuild

Breath-led core and pelvic floor restoration for mothers returning to movement—from six weeks postnatal through to years after birth.

  • Diaphragm and pelvic floor pressure coordination

  • Staged return to running, ocean swimming, and lifting

Perimenopause & Coastal Wellbeing

Targeted strength loading paired with headland mobility and guided breath recovery for women navigating midlife sleep, energy, and joint shifts.

  • Bone-stimulating resistance sets with joint-safe warm-ups
  • Sunrise coastal mobility & barefoot balance sessions
  • Practical protein, hydration, and evening sleep rituals

About Sienna Vale

Eleven years coaching Gold Coast women toward resilient, unhurried strength.

Holding a Bachelor of Exercise & Sport Science from Bond University, Sienna founded her Burleigh Heads private studio to offer women an alternative to crowded, high-fatigue fitness classes.

Every session combines progressive resistance mechanics, APMA-accredited reformer control, and functional breathwork—adapting week by week to life stage, recovery capacity, and coastal routines.
